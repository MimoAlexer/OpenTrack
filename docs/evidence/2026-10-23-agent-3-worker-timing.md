# Bounded worker discovery and timing evidence

A nonempty workspace page without a claim previously returned `Idle`. The foreground loop could therefore wait the full polling interval before every subsequent page. A controlled fixture with 34 workspaces, seven older assignment intents and one workflow replacement reproduced a 43.055-second queue, followed by a 23.592ms successful attempt. The unchanged 30-second completion comparison failed.

The worker now returns `Scanning` for nonempty pages. Foreground Job discovery reserves one credit before each call and permits four calls without a handled outcome per burst: at most four selector calls and 80 candidate claim transactions. Errors and `LostLease` consume credit; `Progress`, `Completed`, `Blocked` and `Retry` reset it. `LostLease` is inactive, so an otherwise inactive round may back off earlier. The configured Job cooldown starts after completion of an exhausted call. Other stages cannot refund it. An empty selector resets the cursor and starts a cooldown; a short or full fourth page can consequently be followed by a second cooldown on the empty lookup.

Each continuation retains the five-stage rotation and one Job visit per round. Budget cooldown preserves the cursor. An inactive round waits until the earlier of its normal interval and the pending Job deadline. Shutdown remains interruptible and drains only the in-flight bounded stage. Production selector SQL, claim order, defaults, authorization, leases, retry rules and schema are unchanged. Continuing pages increases the other stages' potential idle polling frequency; this burst budget covers Job discovery rather than total SQL or CPU.

The existing startup record reports effective interval, pool limit and lease duration. Opt-in worker DEBUG records include closed stage names, monotonic durations and activity/error booleans, plus idle boundaries. Enable them with `RUST_LOG=info,opentrack_server::worker=debug`. `worked` includes scheduling activity; durable receipts establish committed effects. `failed` means a returned error, while handled blocked/dead-letter results have their own durable state. Fields exclude identifiers, credentials, URLs, paths, payloads and raw errors. Clock reads and synchronous logging add overhead. Stage duration includes waits and I/O; PostgreSQL wall-clock correlation is separate. Shutdown can complete a stage without its completion log.
