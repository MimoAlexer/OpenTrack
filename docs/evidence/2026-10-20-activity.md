# Activity history qualification — 20 October 2026

Scope: the remaining activity behavior in [F11](../features/F11_COMMENTS_ACTIVITY.md) and [M04-T03](../milestones/M04_COLLABORATION.md). Existing comments, mentions and subscriptions retain their separately recorded [discussion](2026-10-18-document-editing.md) and [mention](2026-10-18-mentions.md) evidence. Notification delivery is not implemented by this slice.

## Behavior

- Manual issue updates capture actual field differences. Status, priority, estimate, due date and archive state have useful before/after facts; assignee and label changes display states/counts without historical identity lookups.
- Actor UUIDs distinguish accounts with the same display name. Workflow replacement remains attributed to its requester and explicitly identified as a workflow action; assignment cleanup retains system attribution.
- Title and description values are absent from newly written activity metadata. Both bounded and paged history projections also remove legacy content and unknown metadata. Recorded-team authorization still protects historical status names after a move out of a private team.
- Adjacent low-value manual updates group only within two minutes, with at most five events. Expandable details retain all individual facts and timestamps. Important actions and system/workflow entries break groups. Pagination loads 25 durable events, independently of the number of rendered groups.
- A new issue/authentication scope hides old rows immediately. Refresh aborts and invalidates older-page requests, preventing stale content or stale denial responses from replacing current results.
