Full historical item arrays are not loaded into application/client memory just to discard them. The live `CycleIssue` table is independently cursor-paginated and supplies canonical issue ETags; HTTP pagination and ETag parity checks pass.
- The realistic fixture provisions 10,000 synthetic issues and 50 snapshots (500,000 historical facts). The initial join and an initial lateral adjustment hit the unchanged 3-second statement timeout on cold table statistics.
