# Assignment cleanup and durable event consumer — 18 October 2026

## Delivered behavior

Migration 0015 introduces the first required consumer, `assignment_cleanup` version 1. Existing workspaces receive registrations and a durable backfill; new workspaces register atomically with their stream head. Capture writes a receipt and a durable reconciliation job before advancing the checkpoint. Each capture examines at most 100 events. Cleanup examines at most 100 assignments in one team per transaction, checks current eligibility under the workspace guard, and fences every effect against the live lease. Worker scheduling rotates capture, jobs and retention with independent cursors.

Open issues lose an ineligible assignee with an atomic issue version, system activity, audit and event. Activity displays “OpenTrack cleared the assignee because they no longer have access.” Historical completed, canceled, archived and deleted issue assignments remain intact. Manual reopening rejects an ineligible retained assignee unless an eligible assignee or Unassigned is explicitly supplied. Team/workspace restore and workflow changes cause reconciliation. Regranting access or assigning another eligible person before cleanup preserves the current assignment.

Failed capture leaves the checkpoint unchanged, records bounded exponential retries, then a durable dead letter and alert. Restricted operator commands inspect status, retry or reconcile a failed event, resume failed cleanup, and enable/disable capture. Disabling requires drained work and retains the required checkpoint. Reasons and actual operator identity are restricted records; neither appears in generic issue activity. Retention preserves at least seven days and never passes a required consumer checkpoint, including disabled consumers. Deletion, its retention watermark and operation receipt commit together.
